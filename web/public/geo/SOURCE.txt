China / province boundary GeoJSON from Alibaba DataV.GeoAtlas
(https://datav.aliyun.com/portal/school/atlas/area_selector), fetched
2026-10-17 from https://geo.datav.aliyun.com/areas_v3/bound/<adcode>_full.json
and stored at full resolution — the only change is rounding every coordinate to
5 decimals (~1 m), which is far below anything the chart can draw. An earlier
revision Douglas-Peucker simplified these (EPS=0.012 national / 0.014 province)
and the coastlines turned visibly faceted once the map was roamed in, so the
simplification step is gone on purpose: do not re-add it without a very small
tolerance.
See regions.json for the province -> city name and adcode cascade used by the UI.
Note: DataV publishes no district-level boundary for 710000 (台湾省), so that
file is the province outline from <adcode>.json instead of a city feature set.
